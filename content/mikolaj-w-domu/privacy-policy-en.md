_The "Santa at Home" app (Polish name: "Mikołaj w domu") - Last updated: 25.11.2026_

## Controller
The controller of your personal data is Patek (Mariusz Patek), the developer that publishes the app on Google Play. Contact: patek.android@gmail.com

## In short
- The app works entirely on your phone. Photos and videos never leave your phone.
- We do not create accounts and we have no server that collects your data.
- The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads. Google collects the advertising ID and device information for this, and asks for consent in the EEA.

## Photos and videos
Photos taken with the camera, chosen from your gallery or recorded in AR mode are processed only on your phone (for example placing Santa in the photo). They are saved only to your phone's gallery. We do not send them to any server, we do not share them with advertisers, and we have no access to them. A photo or video reaches someone else only if you tap "Share" yourself and pick the app you want to send it with. The app deletes temporary files after saving or when you close the preview.

Permissions: camera (live preview and photo), saving to the gallery, internet access (ads and Google Play Services for AR) and the advertising ID (the AD_ID permission added by Google's ads SDK). The app does not use the microphone or GPS location.

## AR mode (augmented reality)
On phones that support it, Santa and other characters stand on your floor in the live camera preview. The camera image is analysed on your phone; our app does not send it to any server. This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Ads (Google AdMob)
The app is free thanks to Google AdMob ads (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC). We show: a banner at the bottom of the start screen and of the photo or video preview, a full-screen ad after you save a photo or video and when you leave the preview (at most once a minute), an ad when you return to the app after a longer break, and a rewarded ad when you choose to unlock extra characters and scenes for 24 hours. There are no ads during the AR preview. Ads are limited to content suitable for everyone (rating G).

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK on your phone collects and sends to Google, among other things:
- the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
- the IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
- device and system information (model, OS version, language, settings),
- information about ad views and interactions, and diagnostic data (for example crashes and performance).

Google acts as an independent controller of this data and processes it under its own privacy policy. How Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.** On first launch the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can agree to personalised ads or decline; Google then shows non-personalised or limited ads according to your choices. You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy settings" button on the start screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Privacy, Ads).

We do not receive from Google any data that identifies you. We only see aggregated ad statistics (for example the number of impressions).

## Data stored on your phone
The app stores on your phone only the time when the unlock of extra scenes expires (24 hours after you watch a rewarded ad). This information never leaves your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Legal basis and purpose
- Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) to storing and accessing information on your phone and to personalised ads, collected in Google's consent form.
- Our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in funding a free app with ads, for non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention, in line with your choices in the consent form.

## Transfers outside the EEA
Google may also process advertising data in the United States and other countries. Google relies on the transfer mechanisms provided by the GDPR (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses).

## How long we keep data
- Photos and videos: we do not keep them; they stay only on your phone.
- Advertising data: kept by Google for the periods described in its privacy policy.

## Your rights
You have the right to access your data, to rectify it, to have it erased, to restrict processing, to object, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal). You can withdraw consent to ads with the "Ad privacy settings" button. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl).

## Children
The app is intended for adults (parents) who take photos and videos for their children. It is not directed at children. Photos, including photos of children, stay on the phone.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone. Photos and videos saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them.
