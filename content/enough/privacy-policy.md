## 1. Overview

Enough is a simple, offline task manager. All data is stored locally on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with the developer or third parties.

## 2. Data we collect

**We do not collect any personal data.**

All tasks and app settings are stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database. This data never leaves your device.

## 3. Microphone

The app requests access to your microphone solely to enable the speech-to-text feature, which allows you to add tasks by voice.

- Audio is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition engine (provided by the Android operating system / Google).
- The developer does not record, store, or transmit any audio.
- You can use the app fully without granting microphone access — the feature is optional.

## 4. Third-party services

The app uses Android's system speech recognition service. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how that service handles audio data: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 5. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. No personal data is collected from any user.

If the privacy policy changes in the future, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
